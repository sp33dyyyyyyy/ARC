# Sky Climbers (Roblox)

Climb telecom towers and skyscrapers. Climbing burns stamina, platforms let you
catch your breath, and running out means you black out and ragdoll to the
ground. Peaks pay cash and bump your **Tower climbs** counter; spend cash on
boosts to take on the harder, better-paying skyscrapers.

## Open it

**Ready-made place file:** open `build/SkyClimbers.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio and
press **Play**. The island and all six towers are generated by the server when
the game starts, so they appear once you hit Play (edit mode only shows the
spawn pad and lighting).

- A loading screen shows what's being built with a progress bar, then your
  save; it fades out by itself when everything is ready, and SKIP (after a
  moment) drops you straight in while the rest loads in the background.
- To keep the towers in the place for editing, press Play, then in the
  Explorer copy `Workspace.World` and `Workspace.Towers`, stop, and paste them
  into Workspace. The server reuses them instead of rebuilding, so **delete
  them again after updating the game** or you'll keep the old towers.
- Saving progress needs a published place with **Game Settings > Security >
  Enable Studio Access to API Services** on. Without it the game still runs and
  tells you progress won't save.

**From source with Rojo 7:** `rojo build default.project.json -o build/SkyClimbers.rbxlx`,
or `rojo serve` and connect from the Studio plugin.

## Controls: rock climbing

There are no ladders: you climb the towers themselves. On a telecom tower your
hands have to be on an actual beam (a leg, a diagonal brace or a horizontal
girt), so you climb the real lattice, not an invisible wall around it, and you
can swing round a corner leg onto the next face. Skyscraper glass can be
gripped anywhere. Nothing sticks out of a tower to block you; you rest on
yellow rest bars instead.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Walk or jump into a tower | Grab on (SPACE facing a tower works too) |
| W / S, A / D | Climb up / down one move at a time (hold to chain moves), traverse sideways |
| SPACE while climbing | Dyno: leap up to a much higher hold (costs extra stamina) |
| SPACE while falling | Catch the wall (if you're not falling too fast) |
| Q | Let go |
| Stop with your hands on a yellow rest bar | Hang there to recover stamina (also saves a checkpoint) |
| Keep climbing at the top | Climb over onto the summit |
| F | Clip your rope in (carabiner on a telecom beam / suction cup on skyscraper glass); press again higher to reclip |
| X | Unclip your rope |
| V | Rappel down to the ground (needs the Rappel Kit power-up), from a wall or a summit |
| B (or the Power Up Shop) | Shop |
| C | Use chalk (halves drain for 12s) |
| R | Ragdoll / get up |
| P | Back to the plaza |
| G | Emote bar (play any emote you own) |
| O (or the SETTINGS button) | Settings: name tags, camera shake, admin code |
| H (or the CONTROLS tab on the right edge) | Show / hide the controls list |

You climb in moves: reach for the next hold, then pull up. The animation is
Roblox's standard climb animation from each avatar's own animation set, so it
works for every avatar (R15 and R6), never T-poses, and other players see it.
All of these are listed in the **CONTROLS** tab on the right edge of the screen
(H): it slides out a list of every key, and tapping a row does that action. On
touch screens, push the stick toward the wall to climb; the jump button grabs
and dynos, and LET GO, CLIP ROPE and RAPPEL buttons appear while you're on a
wall.

Every button and window makes a keyboard click, and windows (shop, settings,
admin, controls, emote bar) animate open and closed. The click sound is
`Config.Sounds.UIClick`, so you can swap in any sound ID.

**Climbing rope:** the rope is 16 studs long, so as you climb, clip in again
higher (F) like lead climbing. Pressing F makes your character reach up, and
your carabiner (orange D-ring with a silver gate on a blue quickdraw sling)
flies up from your harness and clips round the beam; unclipping (X) drops it
back to your harness. You always get a message saying you're clipped in, or
exactly why not (too far, no beam there, and so on). While you're clipped, wind gusts can't knock you
off, you can hang still on the rope to recover stamina (half the ground rate),
and if you black out or let go the rope catches your fall. Telecom towers: clip
a carabiner round any beam in reach (leg, brace or girt). Skyscrapers: stick a suction cup onto the
glass. It unclips by itself when you climb onto the summit or reach the ground.

**Getting down:** climb down (S), rappel with the Rappel Kit (V), or press P to
go back to the plaza.

## Map

Laid out from the top-down design: the spawn plaza (gate, planter, signposts,
how-to and leaderboard boards) is at the north end, and a dirt path runs south
to the lemon-topped Power Up Shop. Skyscrapers run down the west side and telecom
towers down the east, each in its own fenced lot. Everything is on a fenced,
tree-covered cliff island in the sea.

| Tower | Height | Rest every | Drain | Payout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial Tower (small, easy) | 80 | 20 | 1x | $50 |
| Telecom Tower 1 (medium, normal) | 700 | 50 | 1.3x | $600 |
| Telecom Tower 2 (tall, hard) | 1,050 | 70 | 1.8x | $2,000 |
| Skyscraper 1 (small, easy) | 1,400 | 100 | 1.6x, slippery | $1,500 |
| Skyscraper 2 (medium, normal) | 2,100 | 150 | 2.2x, slippery | $4,000 |
| Skyscraper 3 (large, hard) | 2,800 | 200 | 3x, slippery | $10,000 |

Telecom towers are modelled on self-supporting lattice towers: four tapering
red/white legs, X-bracing, rest bars at every section, dishes, panel antennas,
and blinking aviation lights. Skyscrapers are glass towers with a frame grid,
window-washer rest bars at every rest interval, rooftop AC units, a helipad on
Skyscraper 3, and windows that light up at night.

## Features

- Stamina: crawling and lunging drain it, gripping still drains slowly, standing
  on solid ground recovers it, and the screen loses colour when you're low. At
  zero you black out and ragdoll-fall, with height-based fall damage.
- Boosts: Endurance, Deep Breaths, Quick Hands, Sticky Gloves, Rubber Bones
- Chalk, and a wind gust about every two minutes (with a warning) that can
  blow climbers who are nearly out of stamina off the wall
- Weather: clear skies and rain (no fog). Rain falls around you with lightning
  flashes, and every wall gets slippery (+25% stamina drain).
- Emotes you buy at the Power Up Shop: Wave (free), Point, Cheer, Laugh, and
  Dance 1-3. Play them with G. Your starred summit emote plays automatically
  when you reach a peak. They use the animations built into every avatar, so no
  uploads are needed.
- Checkpoints on rest bars (you respawn hanging on the wall)
- Rappel Kit power-up ($2,500) in the Power Up Shop
- `Tower climbs: N` counter, summit banner and confetti, leaderstats,
  all-time leaderboard board, daily streak reward, unlockable trails
- Day/night cycle with street lights, string lights, lit windows and aviation lights
- DataStore saving with retries, session locking, and no save after a failed load

## Settings, admin panel and gamepasses

**Settings** (O or the bottom-left button): toggle other players' name tags (only
on your screen) and camera shake. Both save with your progress.

**Admin panel:** everyone, including the game's owner, has to enter the admin
password in Settings to open it. The password is `Evan123` (case-sensitive),
set in `src/server/AdminConfig.lua`. That file lives in ServerScriptService, so
players can never see it, but anyone who can read this repository can. The box
shows dots while you type. Wrong passwords are limited to 5 tries every 10
minutes, and every admin action is checked again on the server.

| Admin tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ban / Unban (by username) | Roblox's universe-wide ban, plus the game's own ban list as a backup. Ban needs a second click to confirm. Owners can't be banned. |
| Send to plaza | One player, or everyone in the server |
| Give free / Take away gamepass | Grants or removes VIP, Iron Lungs or Featherweight (saved; player must be in the server) |
| Announcement | Filtered through Roblox's text filter, then shown in every server |

**Gamepasses** (also in the Power Up Shop): VIP (2x summit cash), Iron Lungs
(+50 max stamina), Featherweight (half fall damage). Create the passes on the
Creator Dashboard and put their IDs in `Config.GamePasses` (`GamePassId`) to sell
them for Robux; until then they can only be granted by an admin.

## Layout

```
src/first    LoadingScreen (ReplicatedFirst)
src/shared   Config (all tuning), Rules (pure game logic), Remotes
src/server   Main, WorldBuilder, TowerBuilder, Build, DataService,
             ClimbService, RagdollService, RopeService, ShopService, AdminService,
             AdminConfig (server-only code), EnvironmentService, LeaderboardService
src/client   Main, ClimbController, WallClimb, ClimbAnimator, Emotes, Weather, Effects,
             UI/{Theme,Components,Hud,Overlay,Shop,Settings,AdminPanel}
tests        run.lua (rules unit tests), geometry/ (tower climbability check)
```

## Checks

```
luau tests/run.lua                          # 30 unit tests for the game rules and balance
python3 tests/geometry/run_geometry.py      # runs the real builders against a Roblox mock with
                                            # ray casting, then runs the game's own climbing code
                                            # (WallClimb) up every face of every tower: grab, chain
                                            # moves, rest bars, rope clip beams in reach (and accepted
                                            # by the server's check), traverse, dyno, climb down,
                                            # climb over the summit. Telecom towers are climbed on the
                                            # lattice only (hands checked to be on a real beam),
                                            # including round corners and a full rappel down.
```

Stamina runs on the client, because only the owning client knows its exact
climbing state each frame. That only affects the player's own challenge. Cash,
climbs, purchases, payouts and fall damage are all decided on the server.
